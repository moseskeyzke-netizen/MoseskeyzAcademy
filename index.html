LESSON 1

Beginner lesson: your first major chord

A major triad uses the 1st, 3rd and 5th notes of a major scale. In C major, play C–E–G together.

CC • E • G
FF • A • C
GG • B • D

Try playing each chord slowly with your right hand. Keep your fingers relaxed. Start with C, then F, then G.

WELCOME, BEGINNER

Start your piano journey here

You do not need previous piano experience. Start with the white keys, learn simple major chords, then practise changing between chords slowly.

Your first goal: Play C, F and G major with a relaxed hand. Practise for 10 minutes a day.

GOSPEL STARTER

Try a sweet progression

In C major, begin with this common four-chord pattern:

CI • C E G
GV • G B D
Amvi • A C E
FIV • F A C

Practice tip: Count 1–2–3–4 for each chord. Repeat slowly, then try a steady rhythm.

Make it sound fuller

  • Play the root note in your left hand.
  • Play the chord in your right hand.
  • When comfortable, try chord inversions to move smoothly between chords.
  • Use a metronome and increase the speed gradually.
